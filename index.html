What we do

Clinically Informed Academics,® in practice.

AW Learners is where executive functioning becomes a daily habit. Many bright students stumble not on the content, but on the doing — remembering the assignment, starting it, keeping materials in one place, managing time.

Our clinically informed approach names those skills explicitly and teaches them through real tools. Students don't just hear "get organized" — they build a system in OneDrive, capture their thinking in OneNote, and run their day from Outlook and To Do. The strategy and the software are taught together, so the skill sticks.

It's the same digital fluency employers expect — learned early, and learned with support.
